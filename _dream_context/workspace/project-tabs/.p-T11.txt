## THE PROBLEM (M0)
A census of `window.dispatchEvent` found 13 distinct custom events. TEN of them are
INSTANCE-level — meaningful to exactly one project — yet they are broadcast on `window`, so
with N projects mounted in one realm EVERY listener fires. The worst is
`dreamcontext-delegate-agent`: `lib/delegateAgent.ts:132-136` dispatches SYNCHRONOUSLY and then
reads `payload.accepted` back, and `AgentSurface.tsx:916` is what sets it — so **the first
listener to run claims the delegate**. A task handed to Claude would land in the wrong project
while the right one reported success.
