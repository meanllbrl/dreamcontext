# Mobile screens

The pictures live in `docs/screens/`.

- **Notes list** (`docs/screens/notes-list.png`): every note, newest first. Search and a new-note
  button sit in the top bar; tapping a row opens the editor.
- **Editor** (`docs/screens/editor.png`): the note itself. Every keystroke is saved on the phone at
  once; the chip under the text says whether it is only on this phone or synced everywhere.
- **Sync status** (`docs/screens/sync-status.png`): when the last sync ran, how many changes are
  still waiting to be sent, and a switch to sync now. Opened from Settings.
